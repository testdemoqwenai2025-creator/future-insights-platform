AETH-1 represents a paradigm shift in how humanity interacts with scientific data
at planetary scale. This document outlines the complete architectural vision for
transforming raw binary streams from global sources (satellite constellations,
particle accelerators, sensor networks) into actionable intelligence, peer-reviewed
white papers, and collaborative discovery platforms.

CORE MISSION: Democratize access to frontier science data and empower technically
skilled individuals worldwide to contribute meaningful analysis to global knowledge.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                         VISION STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

"To create an interconnected ecosystem where every byte of scientific data—from
the collision of protons at near-light speed to the spectral signatures of distant
galaxies—flows seamlessly into the hands of curious minds, enabling anyone with
technical skill to extract meaning, publish insights, and advance human understanding."
