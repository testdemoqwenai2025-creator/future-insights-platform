This model built GitHub ($7.5B acquisition), VS Code, Slack, Canva — it works. We're applying it to science. The key insight is that the free tier creates massive adoption and network effects among researchers and students who become our evangelists. When they join companies or labs, they bring AETH-1 with them. The conversion funnel is proven: free users get value, hit usage limits, upgrade to Pro for more power or privacy. Teams need collaboration features. Enterprise needs compliance and security. Our target of 5-10% free-to-paid conversion is conservative compared to GitHub's actual numbers. Even at the low end with our TAM, that's a very large business.
