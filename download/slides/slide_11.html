Projections based on comparable SaaS benchmarks. Upside scenario assumes GitHub-level community adoption could significantly accelerate growth trajectory.

These are conservative projections based on comparable companies in the developer tools and scientific software space. Our base case assumes steady user acquisition through content marketing and community building, with conversion rates in the three to five percent range—conservative compared to GitHub's historical numbers. The key driver is our freemium model which creates a natural funnel from free users to paid subscribers. If we achieve GitHub-level success with similar network effects, these numbers could be significantly higher. Year five revenue of two hundred twenty-eight million dollars would put us in the same valuation range as Datadog at their IPO. The unit economics are particularly attractive because our self-serve model means customer acquisition costs remain low while lifetime value grows as users expand their usage across teams.
