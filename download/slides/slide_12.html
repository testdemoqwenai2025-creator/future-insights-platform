Strategic Priority

China = 20% of initial target market

China isn't an afterthought—it's a core part of our go-to-market strategy from day one. The Chinese government has made scientific self-sufficiency a top national priority through their Fourteenth Five-Year Plan and Made in China twentytwenty-five initiative. They're actively seeking domestic alternatives to Western software, which creates a massive opportunity for us as the first unified scientific computing platform with local presence. With over three thousand biotech companies and government-backed innovation grants available, the market conditions are ideal for rapid adoption. Our strategy is to partner with established Chinese venture capital firms who can provide market access, establish local operations in Beijing or Shanghai, deploy on domestic cloud infrastructure like Alibaba or Tencent Cloud, and secure anchor customers at top universities. This isn't just about revenue—it's about establishing category leadership in the world's second-largest economy before competitors wake up to this opportunity.
